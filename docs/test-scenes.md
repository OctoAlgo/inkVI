# Where we can check things

We test on our own playthrough, on an RTX 5090 and an RTX 5070 Ti under CachyOS with Proton 11. These are the
points in the game we can get to quickly:

- **Prologue, prison:** the intake and canteen cutscenes (close-ups of faces), then Lucia at the cell window. This is
  our benchmark scene (`scene.md`).
- **Prologue, the robbery flashback:** cutscene close-ups outdoors and among cars.
- **Chapter 1, the start:** the boat cutscene and title card, then driving the boat on open water (water, foam,
  buoyancy).
- **Chapter 1, later:** after getting off the boat (the pilot rescue), the Key Lento road, the whale statue crowd.
- **Near the end of Chapter 1:** at sea.

If you'd like something looked at on one of these (a picture problem, a switch or a pull request on an NVIDIA card),
open an issue and say what to look for. We can usually report back with numbers and a description.
